# Code of conduct

checkride follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and focus on
the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not
acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project
space.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/PreethamNoelP). Reports are handled
confidentially. Security vulnerabilities go through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
